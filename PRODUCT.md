# Product

## Register

product

## Users
One Kenyan kiosk (duka) owner, using their own Android phone one-handed at the
counter, often in bright daylight, with a customer waiting. Every session is a
task: ring up a sale, receive stock, log an expense, close the day. Glanceable
numbers matter more than exploration; trust in the money math is the product.

## Product Purpose
DukaSmart is a local-first inventory + sales + daily-close tool for a single
shop. It replaces a paper notebook: know today's sales, cash vs M-PESA, what's
low on stock, and whether the drawer reconciles. Success = the owner closes
every day knowing their numbers and trusting them.

## Brand Personality
Serious merchant tool, quietly confident, precise. Three words: professional,
trustworthy, sharp. The tool should feel like it respects the owner's money —
closer to a bank app than a toy — while staying warm enough for daily use.

## Anti-references
- Default Material "starter app" look (what v1 shipped): seed-color cards on
  grey, centered app bars, stock everything. Ian's verdict: plain and boring.
- SaaS dashboard clichés: gradient stat cards, hero-metric templates, side-stripe
  accent borders, identical card grids.
- Toy/consumer-promo aesthetics: confetti, mascots, gradient text.

## Design Principles
1. **Money is the hierarchy.** The biggest thing on any screen is the number the
   owner came for. Everything else supports it.
2. **Emerald means money-action.** Green is reserved for primary actions,
   success, and money-positive signals — never decoration. Slate neutrals carry
   the UI.
3. **Glanceable under sunlight.** Light surfaces, AA+ contrast, large type for
   totals; readable at arm's length outdoors.
4. **One hand, no waiting.** Primary action reachable by thumb, 48dp+ targets,
   150–250ms feedback, no choreography.
5. **Consistency is trust.** Same button, chip, and card vocabulary on every
   screen; a POS that looks stable feels financially reliable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast minimum on all text; 48dp minimum touch targets; light theme
tuned for outdoor readability; standard system font scaling respected; motion
kept subtle (state feedback only).
